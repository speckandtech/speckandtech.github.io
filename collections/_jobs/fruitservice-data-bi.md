## What awaits you:

* Building, maintaining and developing reports and dashboards in Power BI
* Designing data models and automating processes using Power Query, DAX and Python
* Connecting and integrating various data sources (e.g. ERP, CRM, databases)
* Using cloud services such as Fabric for data analytics and reporting
* Supporting process analysis by providing data and KPIs
* Closely collaborating with business departments to gather requirements and translate them into technical solutions


## What you bring along:

* Degree or vocational training in business informatics, computer science or equivalent qualification
* Strong knowledge of Power BI and Power Query
* Experience with Python for data preparation and automation
* Skills in SQL, DAX and ideally Microsoft Fabric
* Analytical mindset and ability to present complex data in a clear way
* Team-oriented personality with structured and clear communication
* Good command of German and English, Italian is an advantage


## What we offer you:

* Future in a growing and internationally operating company
* Personal and professional development throughout continous training and workshops
* Great working atmosphere in a motivated team
* A modern office in the centre of Bolzano with space for creative ideas
* Benefits such as company events, various conventions, private health insurance and supplementary pension fund
* Health programs, such as Pilates, sponsored lunch in the company cafeteria and company health management
* The proposed starting salary is €30,000 gross per annum (full-time) – with a willingness to offer a higher salary depending on professional experience and qualifications – in accordance with the Collective Agreement for the Retail Sector (CNEL Code: H011) for the Province of Bolzano
