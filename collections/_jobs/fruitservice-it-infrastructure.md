## What awaits you:

* Support IT helpdesk and assistance for employees
* Conducting error analysis, along with resolving hardware, software, and telecommunications system issues
* Provision and setup of new workstations
* Forwarding complex requests (internally or to external service providers) and assisting in problem resolution
* Creation, administration, and maintenance of employee accounts and permissions
* Support in the operation of the existing IT infrastructure and systems
* Contribution to the further development of the IT infrastructure
* Support in IT projects


## What you bring along:

* Completed training/education in the field of IT
* Knowledge of Windows desktop operating systems and Microsoft Office
* Knowledge of networking and Office 365 is an advantage
* Proactive, independent, and solution-oriented way of working
* Service orientation
* Teamwork skills, assertiveness, and commitment
* Good language skills in German - English, and Italian are an advantage


## What we offer you:

* Future in a growing and internationally operating company
* Personal and professional development throughout continous training and workshops
* Great working atmosphere in a motivated team
* A modern office in the centre of Bolzano with space for creative ideas
* Benefits such as company events, various conventions, private health insurance and supplementary pension fund
* Health programs, such as Pilates, sponsored lunch in the company cafeteria and company health management
* The proposed starting salary is €30,000 gross per annum (full-time) – with a willingness to offer a higher salary depending on professional experience and qualifications – in accordance with the Collective Agreement for the Retail Sector (CNEL Code: H011) for the Province of Bolzano
