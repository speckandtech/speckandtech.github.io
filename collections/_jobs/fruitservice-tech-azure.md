Job in Bolzano (IT) or Oberding (DE)

## What awaits you:

* Building and leading a team responsible for the development and administration of cloud applications in Microsoft Azure
* Gathering and analyzing new business requirements in close collaboration with Product Owners from the business departments
* Designing business requirements in close coordination with Technical Leads from ERP, Infrastructure, and Security
* Implementing business requirements in collaboration with Technical Product Owners
* Managing external and internal implementation partners (configuration and development)
* Taking responsibility for the architecture, governance, security standards, and operational management of cloud applications
* Establishing clear processes for Application Lifecycle Management (Build / Run / Change - DevOps)
* Overseeing project deliverables throughout all project phases and ensuring successful implementation with regard to quality, budget, and timeline targets
* Ensuring quality assurance in software development
* Identifying and analyzing performance improvement opportunities

## What you bring along:

* Successfully completed degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or a related scientific discipline
* Extensive experience (minimum 5 years) in the design, implementation, and integration of cloud applications on Microsoft Azure
* Strong expertise in Azure cloud technologies and system integrations, particularly with SAP
* Experience leading technical product teams
* Solid understanding of modern software development methodologies, including service-oriented architectures and agile ways of working
* Good project management skills
* Strong communication skills, team spirit, and excellent organizational abilities
* High level of accountability, initiative, quality awareness, and resilience
* Excellent written and spoken English skills (German language skills are desirable)

## What we offer you:

* Future in a growing and internationally operating company
* Personal and professional development throughout continuous training and workshops
* Great working atmosphere in a motivated team
* A modern office in the centre of Bolzano with space for creative ideas
* Benefits such as company events, various conventions, private health insurance and supplementary pension fund
* Health programs, such as Pilates, sponsored lunch in the company cafeteria and company health management
* The proposed starting salary is €60,000 gross per annum (full-time) – with a willingness to offer a higher salary depending on professional experience and qualifications – in accordance with the Collective Agreement for the Retail Sector (CNEL Code: H011) for the Province of Bolzano
