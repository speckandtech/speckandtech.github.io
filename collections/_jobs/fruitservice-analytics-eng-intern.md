As an intern, you will help transform raw data into meaningful insights that drive decision-making. This is an excellent opportunity for a university student or recent graduate eager to grow in a collaborative environment. 5 Months, Full-Time, On Site.


## What awaits you:

* Enhance Market Analytics campaigns and data projects
* Develop and maintain databases and data systems
* Write and optimize SQL queries for data retrieval and manipulation
* Develop Python scripts for data cleaning, containerization, analysis, and visualization
* Work with Microsoft Fabric for data integration and analytics workflows
* Collaborate with senior team members on various projects
* Participate in the end-to-end analytics engineering process, from data extraction to reporting
* Ensure data quality and improve existing processes and reports
* Assist in digitalization and automation of processes
* Contribute to defining and realizing new data products


## What you bring along:

* Enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program in Computer Science, Data Science, or similar fields
* Understanding of the end-to-end analytics engineering process
* Proficiency in SQL (queries, ER modeling, stored procedures, DML)
* Experience with Python for analytics engineering
* Knowledge of statistical analysis and machine learning concepts
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
* Willingness to learn and grow
* Professional proficiency in English (German or Italian is a plus)


## What we offer you:

* Personal and professional development 
* Great working atmosphere in a motivated team
* A modern office in the centre of Bolzano with space for creative ideas
* Health programs, such as Pilates, free lunch in the company cafeteria and company health management
* Remuneration: €900/month
